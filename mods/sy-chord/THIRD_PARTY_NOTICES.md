## Syntakt Firmware Workbench  SY CHORD implementation

This mod vendors the small generator components needed to recreate the SY CHORD wave bank and its display graphics from a user-supplied stock Syntakt OS 1.41 image. They originate from https://github.com/Bezronczek/syntakt-firmware-workbench, pinned at `49a7a1e5ac1ac4c6869f01b94a820f2b625dada5`.
