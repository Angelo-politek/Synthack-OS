// community firmware mods · Elektron Syntakt · OS 1.42

Pick the mods you want and build your own Syntakt OS, right in your browser.

Every mod is a small, tested patch on the stock OS you download from Elektron. No firmware is hosted here, and your file never leaves your computer.

Create your OS Why SyntHack?

00Why SyntHack

  • Combinable

    One shared structure for every mod. Each one is checked against the stock OS and against the others, so you can use them together.

  • Checked

    The builder refuses a wrong or modified OS, overlapping patches and changes to the recovery sections, then verifies the result before you download it.

  • Open

    Every patch is readable on GitHub, MIT licensed, tested in an emulator against the original code. Anyone can add a mod with a pull request.

    Flash it

    1. Back up your projects and sounds with Elektron Transfer.
    2. Connect the Syntakt over USB, open Elektron Transfer and go to the Drop page.
    3. Drop synthack.syx on it and wait until the update is finished. Don't power off during the update.
    4. To go back, flash the stock Syntakt_OS1.42.syx the same way. If the OS doesn't start, follow RECOVERY.md.
