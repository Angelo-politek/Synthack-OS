01How it works

  1. Your stock OS

    Download the Syntakt OS from elektron.se. The page reads it on your computer and checks that it is the real one.

  2. Your mods

    Select what you want. The page shows the audio CPU your selection uses and adds what the mods need.

  3. Your OS

    The page patches, verifies and gives you one .syx. Flash it with Elektron Transfer. Go back by flashing the stock OS.

02The mods

Each one is a small patch on the stock OS, tested in an emulator against the original code and against the others.

stable tested on the device over many sessions beta works on the device, newer experimental first device tests only

03Before you flash

  • What can go wrong

    A modified OS can fail to start. The boot part of the Syntakt is never touched, so the stock OS can be sent again over MIDI DIN.

  • What the build checks

    Your stock file, every patch against the bytes it expects, conflicts between mods, the boot sections and the unpacking margin. It refuses a result it cannot verify.

  • What you do first

    Back up your projects with Elektron Transfer. Read the recovery procedure. Have a MIDI DIN interface at hand.
