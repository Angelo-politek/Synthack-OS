    Flash it

    1. Back up your projects and sounds with Elektron Transfer.
    2. Connect the Syntakt over USB. Open Elektron Transfer. Go to the Drop page.
    3. Drop synthack.syx. Wait until the update is finished. Don't power off.
    4. To go back, flash the stock Syntakt_OS1.42.syx the same way. OS doesn't start: follow RECOVERY.md.
