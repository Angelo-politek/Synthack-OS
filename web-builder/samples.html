// long samples

Samples

Send samples longer than 5 s to your Syntakt over USB, from this page. Nothing goes to a server.

Needs a build with Long samples and SAMPLER, Chrome or Edge (WebMIDI with SysEx), and Transfer closed. Back up your projects first: this writes the Syntakt's internal memory.

Not connected.
#NameLengthSize

On the Syntakt the long samples come after the stock samples, in this order. Power cycle it to load the changes.
