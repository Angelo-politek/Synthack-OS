// long samples

Samples

Send samples longer than 5 s to your Syntakt over USB, from this page. Nothing goes to a server.

Needs a build with Long samples and SAMPLER, Chrome or Edge (WebMIDI with SysEx), and Transfer closed. Back up your projects first: this writes the Syntakt's internal memory.

Not connected.

#NameLengthVoice memory

On the Syntakt the long samples come after the stock samples, in this order. At boot the Syntakt loads the stock samples, then the long ones until its 64 MiB of voice memory are full: the others stay stored but don't play. Power cycle it to load the changes.
